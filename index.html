About the Project

Where Nature Meets Architecture

Eldeco Echoes of Eden is a premium new-launch residential development at Sector 22D, Yamuna Expressway, crafted for buyers seeking a future-ready address with strong infrastructure upside and elevated everyday living. Positioned close to the Noida International Airport at Jewar and the larger Yamuna Expressway growth corridor, the project benefits from improving regional connectivity, expanding institutional development, and rising end-user demand across this belt. Current market listings describe the project as a low-density development with approximately 5 acres of land and around 558 apartments, while your project brief highlights premium planning, a podium-based layout, and central greens as the core lifestyle differentiators.

Designed around the idea of nature-linked luxury, the project offers 3-side-open corner apartments overlooking 3-acre central greens, creating a sense of openness, cross ventilation, and visual calm that is increasingly rare in high-density developments. The offering is centered on spacious 3 BHK residences in 1550 sq.ft. and 1850 sq.ft. configurations, supported by premium features such as 5 lifts per tower, a podium-based design, a half-Olympic-size pool, and a modern clubhouse environment.
